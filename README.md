# 📌 Project Overview

This project demonstrates an end-to-end **HR Analytics workflow** using four essential data analysis and business intelligence tools:

- **Power BI**

- **Tableau**

- **SQL**

- **Microsoft Excel**

The objective is to transform employee data into meaningful HR metrics, interactive dashboards, and business-oriented insights.

The project focuses on workforce composition, employee attrition, demographics, job satisfaction, education background, and other important HR indicators.

---

# 📂 Dataset

The project uses **HR employee data from 2022** associated with a medical components manufacturing organization.

The dataset contains employee-related information that can be used to analyze:

- Employee demographics

- Department

- Job role

- Age

- Gender

- Education

- Job satisfaction

- Employee status

- Attrition

- Other workforce characteristics

The raw datasets are not included in the public repository to avoid unnecessarily exposing employee-level information.

---

# ❓ Problem Statement

Within an organization, the HR department is responsible for monitoring and managing different aspects of employee data to maintain a healthy and productive workforce.

However, raw employee data alone does not provide an efficient way to monitor important HR metrics.

A major challenge is the lack of clearly defined and consistently measured **Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)** that can help HR teams understand workforce size, attrition, employee demographics, and workforce trends.

This project addresses that challenge by developing structured HR analytics using Power BI, Tableau, SQL, and Excel.

The analysis focuses on questions such as:

- What is the total number of employees?

- How many employees have left the organization?

- What is the employee attrition rate?

- How many employees are currently active?

- What is the average age of employees?

- Which departments have higher attrition?

- How does attrition vary by gender?

- How are employees distributed across different age groups?

- How does job satisfaction vary across the workforce?

- Which education fields have higher attrition?

- How does attrition vary by gender and age group?

# 📈 HR KPIs

The project focuses on several important HR Key Performance Indicators.

## Employee Count

The **Employee Count** KPI provides visibility into the total workforce size.

This metric can support:

- Workforce planning

- Hiring analysis

- Organizational growth analysis

- Workforce capacity planning

---

## Attrition Count

The **Attrition Count** represents the number of employees who have left the organization.

Tracking attrition count helps HR teams understand the scale of employee turnover.

---

## Attrition Rate

The **Attrition Rate** provides a standardized measure of employee turnover.

It can be used to:

- Monitor workforce turnover

- Compare attrition across departments

- Analyze changes in employee retention

- Support further investigation into turnover patterns

---

## Active Employees

The **Active Employees** KPI distinguishes employees who are currently part of the organization from employees who have left.

This provides a clearer picture of the organization's current workforce.

---

## Average Age

The **Average Age** KPI provides an overview of workforce demographics.

This can support analysis related to:

- Workforce composition

- Succession planning

- Recruitment

- Employee development

- Age distribution

---

# 🔍 HR Analysis

The project analyzes employee data across multiple dimensions.

---

## Attrition by Gender

The analysis examines employee attrition based on gender.

This visualization allows HR teams to compare attrition patterns across different gender groups and identify areas requiring further investigation.

---

## Department-wise Attrition

Department-level attrition analysis provides visibility into employee turnover across organizational functions.

This can help identify departments where attrition levels differ and where additional analysis may be useful.

---

## Number of Employees by Age Group

Employees are categorized into different age groups to understand the demographic composition of the workforce.

This analysis can help identify:

- Workforce age distribution

- Concentration of employees within particular age groups

- Potential demographic gaps

- Workforce planning considerations

---

## Job Satisfaction

Job satisfaction ratings are analyzed to provide additional insight into employee experience and workforce satisfaction.

This allows HR teams to examine satisfaction levels alongside other workforce characteristics.

---

## Education Field-wise Attrition

The project analyzes attrition across different education fields.

This provides another dimension for examining workforce turnover and identifying patterns that may require additional investigation.

---

## Attrition Rate by Gender for Different Age Groups

The analysis also examines attrition across combinations of:

- Gender

- Age group

This provides a more detailed view of workforce attrition patterns across different employee segments.
